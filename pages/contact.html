OUR COLLABORATION PROCESS

From Application Review to Full-Speed Production

How RIOPAK partners with your engineering and operations teams from initial concept to turnkey commissioning.

01 / APPLICATION REVIEW

Product & Pack Analysis

We evaluate your product's flowability, bulk density, particle size, and target pouch or bottle dimensions to establish technical feasibility and packaging tolerances.

02 / MACHINE SELECTION

Dosing & Chassis Selection

Our team pairs the ideal dosing mechanism (servo auger, multi-head weigher, or cup filler) with the right intermittent or continuous high-rigidity packaging frame.

03 / PROPOSAL & LAYOUT FIT

Blueprint & Factory Fit

We provide detailed equipment specifications, factory layout integration schematics, power/pneumatic requirements, and transparent commercial proposals.

04 / COMMISSIONING & SUPPORT

Installation & Lifetime Spares

RIOPAK technicians commission your machine on-site, train your operators, calibrate for target accuracy, and provide lifetime support and spares availability.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Packaging Consultation FAQ

Clear answers to common questions about machinery selection, customization, factory visits, and after-sales service.

What products can RIOPAK packaging machines handle?
RIOPAK manufactures packaging machinery for a diverse spectrum of products including spices, flours, salt, pulses, grains, namkeen, potato chips, extruded snacks, dry fruits, cereals, granules, agrochemicals, and industrial materials. Each machine is customized according to your product's bulk density, moisture sensitivity, flowability, and packaging film specifications.
Can RIOPAK machines be customized for our existing factory space?
Yes. Every packaging line can be engineered around your floor dimensions, ceiling heights, raw material infeed hoppers, bucket elevators, and downstream secondary packaging equipment (conveyors, checkweighers, batch coders). We ensure seamless physical and mechanical integration.
Do you provide on-site installation, commissioning, and operator training?
Absolutely. Our experienced service engineers handle complete on-site installation, test-run commissioning with your actual product and packaging films, and train your plant operators and maintenance crew to ensure smooth, dependable day-to-day operation from day one.
Can we visit RIOPAK's facility in Vadodara to inspect machines or test our product?
We welcome prospective clients and factory managers to visit our manufacturing headquarters in Vadodara, Gujarat. You can inspect machines under assembly, discuss engineering drawings with our design team, and perform trial runs using your actual packaging films and product samples.
