Use the Fabrecor Webflow template as the visual reference: large editorial headings, image-led sections, oversized

CONTENT SAFETY
Use only Riopak facts that are verified. Do not carry over the construction-company history, awards, public-company
claims, placeholder statistics or other template residue currently visible on the old Riopak About page.
